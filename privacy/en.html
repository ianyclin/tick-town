Tick-Tock Town — Privacy Policy

繁體中文English日本語한국어Español

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Applies to the website and the iOS and Android apps

In one sentence: Tick-Tock Town collects no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no internet needed to play. Your child's progress stays on their own device and we never see it. The one-time purchase in the app is handled by the App Store / Google Play; we never receive payment or account details.

1. Who makes this game

Tick-Tock Town is made and published by ianyclin. It is a game for children in grades 1–3 (about ages 6–9) to practice reading clocks, setting clocks and working out elapsed time.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or share names, email addresses, addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, location, photos, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data or crash reports. It needs no camera, microphone, location or contacts permission. The Android app includes Google Play's billing library, which brings network permissions used only for purchases (see section 4).

3. What is stored on your child's device

To remember progress, the following is stored only in this device's local storage, readable only by the game on this device and never sent to us or anyone else:

  • answer records (which times were read or set correctly, and which kinds of mistakes happen);
  • settings (language, what to practice, minute ring, music, sound effects, hour chime);
  • daily missions, badges, pet, outfits and how much the town has grown;
  • up to 4 names (nicknames) in "Who's playing", shown only inside the game — please use nicknames, not full names;
  • in the app: whether this device has bought the unlock and how many trial plays each paid game has used (shared by the whole device).

Backups: a grown-up can export these records as a backup file or code in Settings → For grown-ups → Back up records, for moving to another device. Backups are kept only where you choose to save them; the game never uploads them. Importing a backup replaces the records on this device.

4. Internet and third parties

  • All game content, pictures, fonts, music and sounds are packaged in the app (or served with the game page). While playing, the game does not connect to any server and works offline (except for app purchases).
  • In-app purchase (app only): Town Day, What time?, the star map, pet and daily missions are free. Match, Fix-it Shop and Two players can each be tried 3 times, and one purchase then unlocks them all (not a subscription). The purchase screen only appears after a grown-up passes the parent gate. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the game uses their official purchase features (through the open-source Capgo native-purchases package, which sends no data to us or other third parties) to ask the store whether the account on this device has bought the unlock. Payment, account and purchase records are handled by Apple / Google under their own privacy policies; we cannot see them.
  • The game uses no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The only link that leaves the game is this privacy policy, which opens only after a grown-up answers the parent gate. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log the network address of visitors under its own privacy statement. We add no tracking to these pages.

5. Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal data, we do not need or ask for parental consent. The game has no social features, chat, user-generated content or ads. The only purchase in the app is behind the parent gate, and children never see prices.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Records exist only on this device. A grown-up can erase a child's records or delete a player in Settings → For grown-ups (behind the parent gate). Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data also deletes all records. Since we have none of your data, there is nothing to request from us.
